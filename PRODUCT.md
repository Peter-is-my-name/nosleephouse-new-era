# nosleephouse™ — Product Context

## Brand
**nosleephouse™ (nsH)** is a Czech web agency that builds high-performance websites, e-shops, and visual identities for ambitious companies. The name signals relentless work ethic and premium craft.

## Product Purpose
Single-page marketing site (with subpages) that sells web design and branding services to Czech and Slovak business owners. Every section should convert — credibility, proof, desire, action.

## Register
brand

## Users
- Czech/Slovak business owners (SME) who need a new or better website
- Entrepreneurs comparing agencies on quality and trust signals
- Marketing managers evaluating a vendor for a web project
- Secondary: future employees / collaborators checking culture

## Tone
Confident, direct, zero fluff. Czech-language copy, occasionally bilingual (CS/EN switch in header). Smart but not academic. Feels like a conversation with the best developer you know.

## Brand Palette
- `--teal: #1a2f2f` — primary dark teal, main background
- `--ink: #141414` — near-black sections
- `--green: #b2fb58` — lime accent, CTAs, highlights
- `--white: #ffffff` — text, cards
- `--cream: #f7f5ee` — soft light alternative

## Typography
- **Sans**: Inter (variable, via next/font) — headings, UI, nav
- **Serif**: Instrument Serif (via next/font) — italic accent words in headings
- **Body**: Helvetica Neue — body copy

## Strategic Principles
1. Show work first — credibility through portfolio
2. Social proof (testimonials) immediately after portfolio
3. Every section ends with a CTA path
4. Performance matters — next/image, no layout shifts
5. Mobile is equal priority to desktop

## Anti-references
Generic WordPress agency templates, Squarespace aesthetics, rainbow gradients, stock-photo-heavy layouts, corporate blue/grey palettes.
